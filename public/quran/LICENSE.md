# Quran text in this folder

`en.json` is the Quran in Uthmani script (from The Noble Qur'an Encyclopedia, quranenc.com) with the
Saheeh International English translation, taken from quran-json by Risan Bagja Pradana
(https://github.com/risan/quran-json) and stored in a compact form by `scripts/build-quran.mjs`.
